Sausage & Egg Breakfast Pockets

Makes 6

For the filling:
2 tablespoons butter
4 breakfast chicken sausage links, chopped
1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
1/4 red onion, finely chopped
6 large eggs, beaten
1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
Salt and pepper to taste

For assembly:
1 sheet frozen puff pastry, thawed
Flour, for rolling

For the egg wash:
1 egg
1 tablespoon water

Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sausage, bell pepper and onion and sauté until the vegetables are soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Pour in the beaten eggs, sprinkle over the cheddar, season with salt and pepper, and scramble gently until just set, about 2 minutes. Set aside to cool slightly.
4. On a lightly floured surface, roll the puff pastry into a 10x15-inch rectangle and cut it into 6 equal rectangles.
5. Spoon the filling onto one half of each rectangle, fold the other half over, and press the edges closed with a fork. Transfer to the baking sheet.
6. Whisk the egg with the water and brush it over the tops of the pockets.
7. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until puffed and golden brown. Serve warm.
