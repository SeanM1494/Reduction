Weeknight chicken & rice skillet (from my notes app)

INGREDIENTS:
- 1.5 lb chicken thighs, boneless/skinless, cut in 1" pcs
- salt + pepper
- 2 T olive oil, divided
- 1 onion (yellow), diced
- 3-4 garlic cloves minced
- 1 1/2c. long grain rice (rinsed!!)
- 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes w/ juices
- 2 1/2 cups chicken stock OR water + bouillon
- 1 tsp each: cumin, smoked paprika, oregano
- Juice of 1/2 lime
- handful cilantro to finish
- sour cream/greek yogurt for serving (optional)

method: season the chicken all over with salt and pepper then heat 1 T oil in a big deep skillet over med-high and brown the chicken in batches about 5 min, take it out. turn heat down to medium add the rest of the oil + onion, cook til soft ~4 min then garlic 30 sec. stir in rice and the spices and toast it for 2 min so every grain is coated. pour in tomatoes and stock, scrape up the brown bits, put the chicken back on top. bring to a boil then cover, lowest heat, 18-20 min — don't lift the lid!! off the heat rest 5 min covered. squeeze lime over, fluff, top w/ cilantro. sour cream on the side.
