The Only Banana Bread I Make Anymore

Posted September 12 by Marnie · 214 comments · Jump to Recipe

Okay, confession time. When my grandmother moved in with us three winters ago she brought exactly two things from her kitchen: a cast iron skillet and a spotted index card with this recipe on it. I have tested it against every "best ever" banana bread on the internet (my family has eaten a LOT of banana bread) and nothing comes close. The secret, she told me, is that the bananas have to be uglier than you think — practically black.

A few tips before we start! Brown butter is optional but you won't regret it. If you only have two bananas, add a quarter cup of sour cream. And please, please do not overmix.

Grandma's Banana Bread
Prep 15 min · Cook 60 min · Total 1 hr 15 min · Serves 10

Ingredients
3 very ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/2 cups)
1/3 cup butter, melted
3/4 cup light brown sugar
1 egg, beaten
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp baking soda
pinch of salt
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter a 4x8-inch loaf pan.
2. In a large mixing bowl, stir the melted butter into the mashed bananas with a wooden spoon.
3. Mix in the brown sugar, egg and vanilla.
4. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir in.
5. Add the flour last and fold just until no dry streaks remain, then fold in the walnuts.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely.

Notes
Freezes beautifully for up to 3 months wrapped in foil. For muffins, bake 22-25 minutes.
