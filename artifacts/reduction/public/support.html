Support

Need help with Reduction, or found something wrong? Write to admin@recipereduction.com. In the app, Settings › Send feedback fills in your app version for you.

How many recipes are free?

Your first three, however you add them: a link, pasted text, a photo, a page from the app’s browser or Safari’s Share button, or a suggested recipe. A recipe tried on the website before you sign up counts as one of the three. After that, a subscription adds as many as you like: US$1.99 a month or US$19.99 a year in the app. Removing a recipe does not give a free one back.

A recipe site won’t work with a link

Some sites refuse to let our server read them. In the iPhone app, open the page in the Browse tab and tap “Extract this page”, or open it in Safari and share it to Reduction from the Share button. You can also paste the recipe’s text, or take a photo of it.

Something in a recipe came out wrong

Reading a recipe is automatic and can make mistakes, so check amounts, temperatures and times before you rely on them. You can fix a step, rename an ingredient or reorder the cards yourself, and see the recipe’s original wording beside it.

Cancelling a subscription

A subscription bought in the app is managed by Apple: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it. A subscription bought on the website can be managed from Settings on the website.

Restoring a purchase on a new phone

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before; your recipes and subscription come with it. If the app still asks you to subscribe, tap “Restore purchases” beside the subscription options.

Deleting your account

Open Settings in the app or on the website and tap “Delete account”. This deletes your recipes, notes, books and progress, and cannot be undone. It cancels a website subscription straight away; an App Store subscription can only be cancelled by you, as above. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is removed.
